HOW TO PLAY

  • 🕹 Touch & drag left side = move (8 directions). PC: WASD / arrows
  • ⬆ JUMP — double-jump available! PC: Space / W
  • ⚔ ATTACK — sword slash. PC: J
  • 🗡 ROGUE — patrols; stomp or slash it
  • 🪓 BRUTE — charges when it sees you; jump over, then slash
  • 🔮 WISP MAGE — floats up & down; slash or avoid
  • 🔑 Grab the GOLD KEY, then reach the rune GATE to finish
  • 🧪 Red potion heals 1 heart. Chests are bonus loot. Water hurts!
  • ❤️ You have 3 hearts. Lose them all and the forest claims you…
